MediView — UI mockups · 45-day MVP
Desktop Blazor app, hi-fi. One shell, three roles. Study status is the single source of truth and uses your exact labels everywhere — To do, In progress, Re-diagnosis, Done. Colour and type are carried over from your architecture diagrams: navy ink, teal accent, the same four status colours. Eight pages, matching checklist-mvp.json; identifiers, enums and lock facts follow mediview-mvp.dbml. Placeholder data throughout.

Eight pages, nine frames — the 45-day MVP cut. Deferred with the scope they belong to: wallet, cart & checkout, follow-up booking, the roles & permissions matrix, the mobile layout, saved annotations, and notify-me-when-free. The viewport is one page in two states (05 · A and 05 · B), and the report editor with its medication composer lives in that page’s side panel rather than a screen of its own.
