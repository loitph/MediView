MediView — UI mockups
Desktop Blazor app, hi-fi. One shell, three roles. Study status is the single source of truth and uses your exact labels everywhere — To do, In progress, Re-diagnosis, Done. Colour and type are carried over from your architecture diagrams: navy ink, teal accent, the same four status colours. Placeholder data throughout.
